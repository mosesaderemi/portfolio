What I solve

The engineering bottlenecks worth writing software for.

Code is a tool to eliminate constraints. These are the operational and technical problems I systematically diagnose, architect, and resolve for modern businesses.

  1. 01

    Fragile manual workflows & repetitive operations

    Repetitive work done by hand quietly bleeds engineering and operational hours. Teams copy-paste data between CRMs, spreadsheets, and portals; manually dispatch follow-ups; and spend hours formatting reports. A routine task taking 3 hours daily adds up to 780 hours a year nearly 20 full working weeks spent repeating one mechanical cycle, creating delays and human error.

    The operational cost At $20/hour, 780 hours of manual data handling costs $15,600 annually for a single employee. When multiple team members are involved, that cost climbs past $60,000. Autonomous workflow automation (n8n, Python agents, webhooks) handles repetitive tasks instantly with 100% precision.

  2. 02

    Systems that choke under concurrency & leak transactions

    When traffic spikes or databases grow, poorly indexed queries, unoptimized connection pools, and monolithic bottlenecks cause timeouts and dropped requests. Customers arrive ready to purchase or book, hit a spinner, and abandon the session.

    Nobody sends you an invoice for that lost customer, the revenue simply vanishes without a trace.

    The financial impact If 50,000 qualified visitors land on an application and checkout conversion is throttled at 2% due to latency, you capture 1,000 customers. By re-architecting with clean database schemas, caching, and resilient asynchronous job queues, increasing conversion to 5% generates 1,500 additional transactions and $150,000 in recovered revenue at an average order value of $100.

  3. 03

    Siloed platforms & unreachable business intelligence

    Critical operational data ends up scattered across third-party portals, payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay), disconnected SQL/NoSQL databases, and internal spreadsheets. Leadership asks for actionable numbers, but assembling an accurate metric requires hours of manual exports and reconciliation.

    I architect centralized microservices, secure RESTful APIs, and webhook event buses that unify customer records, telemetry, and transactional data into an accessible single source of truth.

  4. 04

    Turning complex AI ideas into reliable production systems

    Building a toy demo with an LLM prompt is straightforward; building an autonomous agent that calls external APIs, queries real databases, enforces strict role-based access control, handles network timeouts, and adheres to strict safety rubrics is an engineering discipline.

    I build production-grade agentic architectures with LangGraph, LangChain, tool calling, and evaluation pipelines that actually work in real-world environments.

Engineering Methodology

I don't start with the code.
